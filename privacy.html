Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy covers Shorts Profit Lab ("the app"), a private publishing tool operated by Zack Martin ("we").

Information we access

When an account owner connects an account and approves access on the platform's own consent screen, the app receives an access token and can access:

  • Profile basics: account name, handle and account ID.
  • Publishing permission: the ability to upload videos, captions and comments to that account.
  • Performance data: views, likes, comments and similar statistics for content on that account.

We do not access private messages, contacts, payment information or passwords. Passwords are always entered only on the platform's own sign-in page.

How we use it

Only to publish content the account owner wants published and to measure how that content performs. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored on a single computer we control, in files readable only by the operator. They are not uploaded to any server or shared.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through these platforms with anyone.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube features use the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while an account stays connected. To disconnect, revoke access in the platform's settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions) or email us. We delete stored tokens and any data obtained through them within 7 days of a request.
